Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Folosure LLC (“Folosure,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, shares and protects information about you when you visit our website, request a demonstration, sign up for a trial, or otherwise communicate with us.

Text messaging, in plain language We only text you if you gave us your number and agreed to be contacted. We never sell your phone number. You can stop the messages at any time by replying STOP.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to information collected through our websites, including demo.folosure.com, our forms, our phone and messaging channels, and our email correspondence. It does not apply to third-party websites we link to.

2. What information do we collect?

  • Information you give us. Your name, business name, phone number, email address, business type, and anything else you choose to send us through a form, call, text or email.
  • Consent records. When you submit a form, we record the date and time and the exact consent wording shown to you, so we can demonstrate how and when permission was given.
  • Technical information. Standard web-server information such as browser type and the pages you viewed.

3. How do we use your information?

  • To respond to your enquiry and to set up and support a trial or account.
  • To contact you by phone, email or text about your request and about our services, where you have given the necessary permission.
  • To improve our website and our services.
  • To meet our legal, tax and record-keeping obligations.

4. SMS and text messaging

By providing your telephone number and submitting one of our forms, you provide prior express written consent within the meaning of the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) for Folosure LLC to contact you at that number by call and by text message, including through automated means, for the purposes described at the point of collection.

5. Do we disclose any information to outside parties?

We do not sell or trade your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who operate our customer database, email and messaging infrastructure on our behalf, and who are contractually required to protect it and to use it only to provide those services.
  • Legal and safety recipients, where disclosure is required by law, or is necessary to enforce our terms or protect the rights, property or safety of any person.

As stated above, text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party for their own marketing purposes.

6. Cookies and analytics

Our demonstration site does not set advertising cookies and does not use browser storage to track you. Our main website may use standard analytics to understand aggregate traffic.

7. How do we protect your information?

Our sites are served over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to our customer database is restricted to people who need it to do their job. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention

We keep enquiry and customer records for as long as needed for the purposes described above and to meet our legal obligations. Records of consent and opt-out are retained for at least four years, which is the limitation period applicable to TCPA claims.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Texts: reply STOP to any message.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
  • Calls: tell us on the call, or contact us using the details below.
  • Access, correction and deletion: contact us and we will action reasonable requests, subject to our legal obligations.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act or the California Consumer Privacy Act. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

10. Children’s privacy

Our services are intended for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top will always show the current version.
